Staff-facing direction

White and warm neutral surfaces, fine separators, compact rows and selected-person context. Color carries meaning. Buttons are flat; decoration stays secondary to the work.

Resident-facing direction

Gentle original line drawings, one instruction at a time, large clearly labelled choices and immediate reassurance when someone declines. Recording and sending are explained separately.
